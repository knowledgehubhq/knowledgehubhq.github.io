About Everyday Tech Help

Everyday Tech Help is a free resource of plain-language technology guides. It exists for one reason: to answer the everyday technology questions that friends, parents, and coworkers ask — the kind of questions that shouldn't require a computer science degree to understand.

Who publishes this site

Everyday Tech Help is published by the team behind Topics Quest, a website of practical how-to guides. The same editorial standards apply here: original writing, practical steps, and no filler.

Editorial standards

  • Original content. Every guide is written from scratch for this site.
  • Tested steps. Instructions reference real menus and settings on current iPhone and Android devices.
  • No fake authority. We don't invent expert quotes, statistics, reviews, or testimonials.
  • No keyword stuffing. Articles are written for readers first.
  • Free forever. No paywalls, no required signups, no tracking.

Corrections

Technology changes fast. If you spot an outdated step or an error, please contact us and we'll fix it.
